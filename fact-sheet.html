October 1 to 3, 2026 · Historic Clearview Golf Course · East Canton, Ohio
In 1946 and 1986, two organizations were created that changed the historical landscape of golf in the United States. In 2026 they celebrate 120 continuous years together.
William J. Powell
Clearview · 1946

An Army veteran barred from public courses, he built Clearview by hand: the first course in America designed, built, and owned by a Black man. Run by the Powell family for 80 years.

Harold Dunovant
Hall of Fame · 1986

The first Black graduate of the PGA Business School, he founded the NBGHF to recognize Black golfers. Led today by his son Jeff, the only Black father and son who are Class A PGA members.
